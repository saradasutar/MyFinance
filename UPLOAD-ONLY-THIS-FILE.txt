MYFINANCE SINGLE-FILE FIX

1. Open https://github.com/saradasutar/MyFinance
2. Click index.html.
3. Click the pencil icon (Edit this file).
4. Press Command+A inside the editor and delete the old code.
5. Open the supplied index.html with TextEdit in plain-text mode, copy everything, and paste it into GitHub.
6. Click Commit changes, then Commit changes again.
7. Wait 1–3 minutes and open https://saradasutar.github.io/MyFinance/?v=4
8. Press Command+Shift+R.

The design is now inside index.html, so styles.css and app.js are no longer required.
For login/data features, replace PASTE_YOUR_GOOGLE_APPS_SCRIPT_WEB_APP_URL_HERE inside index.html with your Apps Script /exec URL.
